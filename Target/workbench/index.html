SIMULATION BOUNDARY

Real on this page: the classifier rules (first match wins, per README.md:153-176), the preview line shapes (README.md:108-133), the threshold semantics and defaults (README.md:395-420). Simulated: the BLAKE3 digest (a deterministic stand-in hash - the real 40-char digest needs the Rust store), the 40-123 ns timing readout, and aphrodite_retrieve (shown as the decision text, never executed).
