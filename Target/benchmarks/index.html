Benchmarks

These figures come from the project's own benchmarks and from a study of the author's real Hermes sessions. Benchmark ratios are measured; session savings are estimated, and the uncertainty band is shown below.

MAX COMPRESSION
610×

Best case on large low-entropy prose. 132× overall on the same corpus, 106 KB → 800 B. The best case is not part of the per-type chart below - that chart peaks at 416×. SRC: README.md:12, 446

END-TO-END
8-40 ms

Including the HTTP round-trip; the headline figure is ~10 ms. SRC: README.md:460, 12

CLASSIFICATION
40-123 ns

The compress path itself stays under 1 ms. SRC: README.md:461, 133; docs/README.md:7

MEDIAN
23×

Fewer tokens on tool output - the median the README reports for its per-type table. SRC: README.md:459
